How to play: Use your arrow keys to move the tiles. When two tiles with the same rank touch, they get promoted!

Boot camp starts with a private at 2 and ends with a general at 2048; between them sit corporal, sergeant, lieutenant, captain, major, colonel and the three grades of general. Two badges of the same rank on the 4×4 board merge into the next one, and the points that promotion is worth go straight to your score. Arrow keys move the whole parade, or you can swipe on a phone. A board with no free square and no matching neighbours ends the campaign, so keep a corner open and plan one promotion ahead. Put the general on the board to win.
